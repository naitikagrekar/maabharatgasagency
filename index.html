SAFETY FIRST

Use LPG safely.
Every day.

A few simple habits can help keep your home safer when using LPG.

01

Keep the kitchen well ventilated while cooking.

02

Turn the regulator off when the gas is not in use.

03

Keep flammable items away from the cooking area.

04

For a suspected gas leak, avoid flames and electrical switches, move to a safe place and contact your LPG emergency service/provider.
